Five steps to switch

  1. Check your Arc version

    Open Arc, then choose Arc → About Arc in the menu bar. It should say 1.170.0 or 1.169.0. The installer stops if it's anything else, so it never builds something that wasn't tested.

  2. Quit Arc completely

    Press ⌘Q in Arc. Closing the window isn't enough. The installer takes a clean copy of your data, and that only works while Arc is closed. Save anything you're typing first.

  3. Run one command

    Open Terminal (⌘Space, type “Terminal”), paste this and press Return:

    curl -fsSL https://arcreborn.cool.computer/install-artbrowser.sh | bash

    It checks your Mac, backs up your Arc data, builds ArtBrowser in your Applications folder and leaves your original Arc untouched. It takes about a minute. Prefer a file? Use Download installer above, then run bash ~/Downloads/install-artbrowser.sh.

  4. Open ArtBrowser and allow Keychain access

    Open ArtBrowser from your Applications folder. macOS will ask to use “Arc Safe Storage”. Click Always Allow, because that's what unlocks your saved logins and cookies.

  5. Keep Arc installed for now

    Don't delete Arc or its Keychain entry “Arc Safe Storage”. Your logins depend on that entry. ArtBrowser is a one-time copy, and changes in either browser do not sync to the other.

The honest list

What works, and what doesn't

Works

  • Your Spaces, folders, pinned and open tabs
  • History, bookmarks and saved passwords
  • Your extensions
  • Website logins that were saved on your Mac

Turned off

  • Arc account, sync and sharing links
  • Arc Max and other AI features
  • Automatic updates, including browser security fixes
Please read: the engine inside is a frozen copy of Chromium 155. It won't get security updates, so be careful on sites you don't trust and keep macOS up to date. For banking and other sensitive accounts, use a browser that is still maintained. The official route from the Arc team is Dia, which imports your Arc data.

ArtBrowser is an unofficial community project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Browser Company. “Arc” and “Dia” belong to their owners.

It doesn't include or download Arc. The installer only copies the Arc app and data that are already on your own Mac, and it never changes the originals.
